# Community Feedback

## Philosophy

Community Feedback exists to strengthen local businesses and help them better serve the Kahawa West community.

Unlike traditional review systems that focus primarily on ratings, KWEST encourages residents to share constructive experiences that help businesses grow while helping neighbours make informed decisions.

Our belief is simple:

> **KWEST verifies businesses. The community strengthens them.**

Community Feedback is not a complaint box.

It is a growth tool.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Respect First

Every piece of feedback should be respectful, honest and constructive.

We are building a stronger community, not encouraging online arguments.

---

## 2. Improvement Over Judgment

Instead of asking residents to judge businesses, we ask them to help businesses improve.

---

## 3. Experiences Matter

Residents share experiences.

Businesses discover opportunities.

The community benefits.

---

## 4. Reputation Must Be Earned

No business receives reputation badges by default.

Recognition is earned through genuine community experiences over time.

# Vision

KWEST is not building another review platform.

We are building a neighbourhood improvement platform.

Every experience shared should make businesses stronger, residents more informed and Kahawa West a better place to live, work and shop.

Every click should strengthen the relationship between residents and local businesses.

If a feature creates more trust, keep it.

If it creates more noise than value, rethink it.
